Grammar focus

Simple Present with do / does

Use he/she/it + verb+s in the affirmative. Everywhere else, use do or does as auxiliaries.

AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / You / We / They studyI / You / We / They don't studyDo I / you / we / they study...?
He / She / It studiesHe / She / It doesn't studyDoes he / she / it study...?

Adverbs of frequency

Adverbs of frequency tell us how often something happens.

AdverbHow often (%)
always100%
usually~90%
often~70%
sometimes~50%
rarely~10%
never0%

Where does the adverb go?

1. Before the main verbShe always studies at night.
2. After the verb to beShe is often tired.
3. Sometimes/usually can also start or end a sentenceSometimes I study at night. / I study at night sometimes.
4. Longer expressions go at the endI check my email every day.

Other frequency expressions

Not every frequency word is a single adverb — these expressions go at the end of the sentence.

every day every morning once a week twice a month on Mondays on weekends from Monday to Friday

My Daily Routine

Hi! I'm Sofía, and I'm a Nursing student at UNLPam. Every day, I wake up at 6:30 and I have a quick breakfast. I usually walk to the faculty because it isn't far from my house. I have classes in the morning, and I have lunch with my classmates at noon. In the afternoon, I do my practical hours at the hospital. I don't work in the evenings — I usually study or relax at home. I sometimes go to the gym, but I never skip my Nursing classes. I go to bed around 11 p.m.
